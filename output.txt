### Quick Take‑aways
- **Title & Timing:** *Avengers: Doomsday* will hit theaters on **18 December 2026**, positioned as the MCU’s multiverse finale.  
- **Directors:** The Russo brothers return, bringing their experience from the original Avengers trilogy.  
- **Star Power:** Robert Downey Jr. returns as a brand‑new villain—Doctor Doom—while a broad ensemble of legacy Avengers, Wakandans, Fantastic Four and X‑Men characters converge.  
- **Story Hook:** The multiversal “Doomsday” engine threatens reality; the combined forces of Earth’s greatest heroes must stop Doom and resolve the lingering threat of Kang.  
- **Future Outlook:** The film is expected to lay the groundwork for the upcoming “Secret Wars” crossover and the next phase of MCU storytelling.
